## 1. Executive Brief & Project Goals

### 1.1 Purpose of This Document
This document is the complete specification for the Vectrae Infotech website. It defines every page, its purpose, required sections, content, SEO metadata, and design guidance. Use this as the single source of truth for design, development, and content population.

### 1.2 Business Objectives
- Generate qualified B2B enterprise leads across all six solution verticals
- Position Vectrae as the most credible, full-spectrum enterprise technology solutions provider in India
- Clearly differentiate Vectrae from competitors (e.g., Team Computers) through richer service depth, stronger OEM partner roster, and industry proof
- Achieve Page 1 Google rankings for target enterprise IT keywords in India
- Reduce sales cycle time by giving prospects all the information they need online
- Enable easy contact & RFQ submission from every page

### 1.3 Competitive Context
Primary competitor Team Computers has a clean, functional website with ~25–30 pages. Vectrae's website must surpass it in three dimensions:

| Dimension | Vectrae Advantage to Deliver |
|---|---|
| Depth of Services | 7 solution verticals with detailed sub-service pages vs Team Computers' 6 broad categories |
| Proof / Trust | 49 named clients, 43 OEM partners, case studies, industry-specific pages |
| Lead Generation | RFQ forms, callback widgets, WhatsApp CTA, quote builder on every page |
| SEO | Dedicated pages per service + industry = 40+ indexable, keyword-optimized pages |
| UX / Premium Feel | Modern enterprise design: dark navy + orange, animated counters, smooth scroll |

### 1.4 Company At a Glance

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Clients Served | 2,300+ |

| OEM / Technology Partners | 43 |
| Named Enterprise Clients | 49 |

| Ghost / Link | No border \| Navy text \| underline on hover |
| Hover State | Darken fill by 10% \| Lift shadow \| smooth 200ms transition |

### 12.4 Page Layout Rules
- Max content width: 1280px, centered
- Section padding: 80px top/bottom desktop, 48px mobile
- Card border radius: 12px with subtle box shadow (`0 4px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`)
- Section backgrounds alternate: White → Light Blue (#EBF3FB) → White → Navy (for CTAs)
- All images: WebP format for performance
- Animations: Subtle fade-in on scroll (use AOS.js or Framer Motion) — no aggressive animations
- Sticky header: Nav stays fixed on scroll with slight blur background
